Note: the numbers in these filenames are just labels — the order
tracks appear on the site is controlled by js/tracks.js, not by the
filenames, so files never need renaming when the order changes.

Other notes:
- MP3 (audio/mpeg) is the safest format for both the browser <audio>
  element and Chromecast's default receiver.
- Chromecast loads media directly from the receiver device, so files
  must be reachable at a public HTTPS URL — this only works once the
  site is deployed (it will NOT work when just opening index.html
  locally on your computer).
- Track durations in js/tracks.js are placeholders ("--:--") — the
  site auto-detects the real length from each file's own metadata
  once it's reachable, so you don't need to fill these in by hand.
- To rename tracks, reorder them, or change titles, edit js/tracks.js
  — the tracklist and player are generated from that file.
- Delete this file once your audio is in place.
