Drop your MP3 masters in this folder using the filenames already
referenced in js/tracks.js:

  01-the-timberline.mp3
  02-pdx-in-the-mist.mp3
  03-anywhere-any-day.mp3

Notes:
- MP3 (audio/mpeg) is the safest format for both the browser <audio>
  element and Chromecast's default receiver.
- Chromecast loads media directly from the receiver device, so files
  must be reachable at a public HTTPS URL — this only works once the
  site is deployed (it will NOT work when just opening index.html
  locally on your computer).
- Track durations in js/tracks.js are placeholders ("--:--") — update
  them to match your actual files once you know the exact runtimes.
- To rename tracks, reorder them, or change titles/durations, edit
  js/tracks.js — the tracklist and player are generated from that file.
- Delete this file once your audio is in place.
